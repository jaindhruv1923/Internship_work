WITH StudentCompoundMetrics AS (
    SELECT 
        u.ukid, u.enrollment_no, att.overall_attendance_pct,
        COALESCE(fd.total_overdue, 0) AS overdue_fees,
        COALESCE(hb.curfew_count, 0) AS curfew_breaches
    FROM users u
    INNER JOIN ams_attendance_summary att ON u.ukid = att.student_ukid
    LEFT JOIN (SELECT student_ukid, SUM(balance_amount) AS total_overdue FROM academic_fee_dues GROUP BY student_ukid) fd ON u.ukid = fd.student_ukid
    LEFT JOIN (SELECT student_ukid, COUNT(*) AS curfew_count FROM hostel_curfew_breaches GROUP BY student_ukid) hb ON u.ukid = hb.student_ukid
)
SELECT COUNT(*) AS total_cohort, SUM(CASE WHEN overall_attendance_pct < 65 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS red_zone_count
FROM StudentCompoundMetrics;

              # PRD: Proactive Student Retention, Administrative Intelligence & B2B2C Experience System (Digii Campus 2.0)
            

            

              **Document Owner:** Dhruv Jain (Data & Product Intern)  
              **Target Title:** Product Intern (Enterprise Intelligence & Core ERP)  
              **Reviewers:** Mr. Suraj Bhatt (Engineering & BI Manager), Saksham Gupta (Principal Engineer)  
              **Status:** APPROVED / IN PRODUCTION PROTOTYPE  
            

            
## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

            

              Digii Campus (CollPoll) manages operational data for hundreds of thousands of students across premier higher education institutions. However, legacy ERP operations store data in disparate modules (Academics AMS, Finance Fees, Turnstiles, Residential Life, Campus Health).
              When a student enters academic or financial jeopardy, the signals manifest sequentially across 3 to 5 separate modules, yet remain invisible to decision-makers until the student officially defaults or drops out.
            

            
## 2. Core Value Proposition: Dual-Value B2B2C Architecture

            

              - **University Value (B2B):** Protects ₹1.84 Cr in at-risk tuition per semester, automates 40 hrs/week of manual finance reconciliation, and eliminates academic scheduling clashes.  
              - **Student Value (B2C):** Eliminates 14-day physical paper clearance queues, resolves homework burnout, provides 100% verified auto-resumes, and delivers phone-only campus life.
            

            
## 3. Database Architecture & Non-Functional Specifications

            

              - Target Replica: `collpoll_10gb_bi_dump` (1,647 tables, MySQL 8.0 RDS Aurora).  
              - Covering Indexes: `student_ukid`, `status`, `punch_timestamp`, `balance_amount`.  
              - Query SLA: p99 latency < 85ms across 4,850 student cohort.  
              - Blast Radius: Canary rollout (10% to 100%) with automated circuit-breaker auto-rollback.
